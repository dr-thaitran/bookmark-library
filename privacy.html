Privacy Policy

Bookmark Library (Chrome extension) · Effective 10 November 2026

Bookmark Library shows your Chrome bookmarks as a library on your New Tab page. It is built to keep your data on your own devices. The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data. The extension has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

What the extension reads

  • Your bookmarks (titles, addresses, folders, date added and date last opened) — to draw the shelves and cards. It reads them through Chrome; it does not copy them anywhere else.
  • Images you choose as shelf covers.
  • Only if you turn previews on and allow site access:
    • the web pages you have bookmarked, to read their preview image and description (the standard og:image and description tags). These requests are sent without your cookies.
    • a snapshot of a page you have bookmarked while it is open in your active tab. Pages that are not bookmarked are never captured.
  • What you type in the search box — used to filter your bookmarks on the page. When you press Enter, it is passed to your own default search engine in Chrome (or opened as a web address), exactly as if you had typed it in the address bar.

Permissions

  • Bookmarks — to show your bookmarks as shelves and cards.
  • Storage / unlimited storage — to keep covers, settings and preview images.
  • Favicon — to show each site's icon, from Chrome's own icon cache.
  • Search — to send a search to your default search engine when you press Enter.
  • New Tab page — the library replaces Chrome's New Tab page.
  • Access to websites (optional) — not granted at install. Chrome asks you only if you turn previews on. You can remove it at any time in the extension's settings (⚙) or at chrome://extensions.

Your choices

  • Turn previews and snapshots off, or remove site access, from ⚙ in the library.
  • Delete all cached previews with “Clear preview cache” in ⚙.
  • Remove a shelf's cover (the ↺ button on the shelf) to delete it and its synced thumbnail.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored on that computer.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Any change that would let data leave your devices would require your consent first.
