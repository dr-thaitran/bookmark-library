Bookmark Library

A Chrome extension that turns your New Tab page into a library: bookmark folders become shelves, and links become catalogue cards.

Common questions

My New Tab page didn't change.

When you install, Chrome asks whether to keep the new New Tab page. If you chose “Change it back”, open chrome://extensions, turn Bookmark Library off and on again, and choose Keep it.

How do I make it the first page when Chrome starts?

Chrome Settings → On startup → Open the New Tab page.

Why do some cards show only an icon?

Website previews are optional. Turn them on from ⚙ in the library. Previews then appear as the extension loads your bookmarked sites, and as you visit them.

How do I change a shelf's cover?

Hover over the shelf and click the image button, or drag a picture onto it. The ↺ button returns to the automatic cover.

Do my bookmarks sync?

Yes. The extension shows Chrome's own bookmarks, so Chrome Sync keeps them the same on all your computers. Your settings and shelf covers sync too. On a new computer, previews need a one-click permission the first time.

How do I remove it?

Right-click the extension's icon → Remove from Chrome. Your bookmarks are not affected.
